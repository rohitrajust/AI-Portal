Socria

A personalized learning experience for AI and software terms. Instead of just handing you a definition, it first asks what you already think a term means -- then builds on or corrects your understanding.

✨ What It Does

  • ❓ You ask about any AI/software term
  • 🔄 It asks what you currently understand first
  • 🧩 It builds on what you got right
  • ✅ It gently corrects what's off
  • 📈 The more you engage, the more it teaches

How to Use

1️⃣ Ask

Type in any AI or software term you want to understand, e.g. "What is a token?"

2️⃣ Share Your Understanding

The tutor asks what you already think it means -- answer in your own words.

3️⃣ Get Corrected & Taught

It builds on what you got right, and clears up what wasn't quite there.

4️⃣ Keep Going

Ask a follow-up or a new term -- each round deepens your understanding.

Best Practices

💭 Answer Honestly

Give your real understanding, even if you're unsure -- that's how it corrects you accurately.

🔂 Ask Follow-ups

Dig deeper on anything that's still unclear.

📝 Try Related Terms

Learning connects better when you explore related concepts too.
