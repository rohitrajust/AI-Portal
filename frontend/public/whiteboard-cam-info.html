VisionBoard

During a whiteboard session, an instructor might sketch and write several things across the board. Upload a photo of that whiteboard, and get back a clean, organized diagram -- ready to review and share.

✨ What It Does

  • 📷 Reads a photo of a whiteboard
  • 🧠 Understands the drawings, text, and flow shown
  • 🗂️ Organizes it into a clean structure
  • 🔀 Produces a flow diagram or canvas view
  • 📐 Removes the mess of a real whiteboard session

How to Use

1️⃣ Capture

Take a clear photo of the whiteboard at the end of the session.

2️⃣ Upload

Upload the photo to the application.

3️⃣ Generate

The app reads the content and builds an organized diagram.

4️⃣ Review

Check the generated canvas or flow diagram.

5️⃣ Save or Share

Keep the clean version instead of the original messy photo.

Best Practices

📷 Capture the Whole Board

Make sure nothing is cropped out of frame.

💡 Good Lighting

Avoid glare or shadows across the writing.

✍️ Write Legibly

Clear handwriting on the board leads to a more accurate result.
