Speak It. Get The Document.

EchoDocs

An AI-powered application that listens to you describe what you need out loud, and turns your words into a fully written, professional business document -- ready to review and download.

✨ What It Does

  • 🎤 Record your requirements using voice
  • 📝 Convert speech into text automatically
  • 📄 Identify the right document type
  • 🤖 Generate a professionally structured document
  • ✏️ Review and edit before finishing
  • ⬇️ Download as a Word (.docx) file

How to Use

1️⃣ Launch

Open the Voice Document Generator in your browser.

2️⃣ Record

Click "Start Recording" and speak naturally about the document you need.

3️⃣ Stop & Review Layout

Click "Stop." The app suggests a document structure based on what you said -- edit anything you like.

4️⃣ Generate

Click "Generate Document" to have the full document written out for you.

5️⃣ Download

Review the finished document, then download it as a .docx file.
